Key Benefits of Solar Control Window Film

  • ✦ Reduce solar heat up to 80% — The film blocks the infrared radiation that heats up spaces, keeping the interior up to 10°C cooler.
  • ✦ Block up to 99% of UV rays — Protect your skin, furniture, upholstery, wood floors, and artwork from fading caused by ultraviolet radiation.
  • ✦ Save on AC bills — By reducing heat gain, your AC units work less. Energy savings can pay back your investment in a short time.
  • ✦ Protect furniture and flooring — Avoid premature fading of fabrics, curtains, rugs, and wood flooring caused by the intense Caribbean sun.
  • ✦ Increase privacy — Mirrored models provide excellent daytime privacy from the outside without sacrificing your view from the inside.
  • ✦ Maintain views from inside — Unlike heavy blinds or curtains, the film lets natural light in and keeps the view open to the outside.

How does solar control window film work?

Without protection, most of the sun's energy passes through the glass and heats up the interior. 3M™ film reflects and absorbs this energy before it enters, reducing the load on your air conditioning and lowering CO₂ emissions.

3M™ Window Film Product Lines

We work with the official 3M architectural window film portfolio to choose the exact option for your glass type, sun orientation, and privacy needs.

3M Prestige

The premium metal-free line. Multi-layer nanotechnology that rejects heat while remaining virtually transparent. Does not interfere with cell phone or WiFi signals. Ideal for residences, hotels, and offices with spectacular views.

3M Ceramic

Color-stable nanoceramic technology: does not fade or change color over time. Excellent heat reduction while keeping the natural look of the glass. Great for retail stores and offices in Cancun.

3M Neutral

Uniform neutral gray tone with a classic corporate aesthetic. Balance between natural light transmission, heat reduction, and privacy. The proven choice for office buildings.

3M Night Vision

Low interior reflectivity: keep your clear night views looking outside without the annoying mirror effect. Warm tone and scratch-resistant coating. Perfect for oceanfront homes and restaurants.

3M Silver P18

The classic mirrored film for glass facades: maximum daytime privacy and the highest solar heat rejection. Appears mirrored from the outside, with clear visibility from the inside.

3M Prestige Exterior

Installed on the exterior side of the glass: the perfect solution for hard-to-reach windows, double-height voids, and special glazing. Same Prestige performance, applied where others cannot reach.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much heat does solar control window film reduce?

Depending on the film model, solar heat reduction ranges from 40% to 80%. High-performance options like 3M Prestige, Ceramic, or Silver offer the highest reduction. In Cancun's tropical climate, this results in a noticeable temperature drop and substantial electricity savings.

Does it darken the glass?

It depends on the model. We offer virtually clear films like 3M Prestige 60 or 70 that block heat and UV without visibly changing the glass, as well as darker or mirrored films like Silver P18 for privacy. We will advise you on the best light transmission level for your space.

What is the difference between 3M Prestige, Ceramic, and Silver?

Prestige is a premium metal-free film: maximum clarity and no signal interference. Ceramic uses color-stable nanoceramic technology for a natural look and great heat rejection. Silver is mirrored: maximum daytime privacy and highest heat rejection, ideal for glass facades. We recommend the correct line based on your space and budget.

Does 3M film interfere with cell phone or WiFi signals?

Metal-free films like 3M Prestige and Ceramic do not interfere with cell phone, WiFi, GPS, or radio signals. Traditional metallic films may slightly weaken signals; we will help you choose the best film based on your connectivity needs.

How long does the installation take?

Installation in a standard room or condo can be completed in a few hours. Larger residential or commercial projects can take several days. It is a clean, non-invasive process with no construction required. The film fully cures within 24 to 48 hours.

Can it be installed on any type of glass?

Solar control film is compatible with most glass types: single-pane, tempered, laminated, and double-glazed. We perform a pre-evaluation to recommend the correct film. For hard-to-reach interior windows, we offer the 3M Prestige Exterior line for outside installation.
