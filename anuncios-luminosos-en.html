Light boxes provide a continuous illuminated graphic, while illuminated letters give each character its own shape. Send your logo, approximate sign dimensions, a photo of the facade, and the installation address. Include the mounting height and whether electrical power is already available near the sign.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an illuminated sign cost?

The quote depends on dimensions, lighting, materials, fabrication complexity, and installation access. Send a facade photo and approximate measurements so we can define the scope.

How long do LED signs last?

Service life depends on the LED modules, power supply, operating hours, exposure, and maintenance. We select components for the installation and discuss maintenance needs when quoting.

Do you repair existing signs?

Yes. We provide preventive and corrective maintenance, including LED modules, power supplies, and cleaning. Send photos and describe the fault so we can assess the work required.

How long does fabrication take?

A standard sign typically takes 5 to 10 business days after design approval and deposit. Larger or more complex structures may take longer. We confirm the schedule in your quote.
