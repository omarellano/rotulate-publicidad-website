Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to wrap a car or van in Cancun?

The price depends on the vehicle size, the coverage (partial or full wrap), and the design complexity. A partial wrap is more affordable than a full wrap. We quote for free in under 24 hours: send us photos of the vehicle via WhatsApp at +52 998 400 7987 and we will give you an accurate estimate.

Will a vehicle wrap damage my car's paint?

No. When installed and removed correctly with premium vinyl (3M, Avery Dennison, Orafol), a wrap actually protects the original paint from scratches, sun, and wear. The paint should be in good condition and free of rust at the time of installation. When the vinyl is removed years later, the paint stays intact.

How long does a vehicle wrap last?

A quality vinyl professionally installed lasts between 3 and 7 years, depending on the material brand, sun exposure, and maintenance. We work with premium 3M and Avery Dennison vinyl that delivers maximum durability even under the Mexican Caribbean climate.

Do you print banners and large-format graphics?

Yes. We print banners, mesh, vinyl, and large-format graphics on HP Latex eco-friendly technology with vivid color and high durability. For urgent jobs we offer express printing with 24-hour delivery in Cancun. Send us your file or idea via WhatsApp for a fast quote.

What are the benefits of 3M solar control window film for buildings?

Solar control window film for buildings and homes reduces up to 80% of the heat entering through the glass, blocks up to 99% of UV rays, saves on air conditioning costs, protects furniture and floors from fading, and increases privacy. Ideal for the Caribbean climate. See our 3M window film service.

What is alucobond and what is it used for on a facade?

Alucobond is an aluminum composite panel, lightweight and durable, used to renovate commercial facades with a modern, professional finish. It is weather resistant, easy to maintain, available in multiple colors and finishes, and faster to install than a traditional remodel. Perfect for stores, offices, malls, and corporate buildings in Cancun.
