BEFORE YOU ORDER

Clear details, from the start.

What is the cutoff for same-day printing?

Order and pay before 11:00 a.m., Cancun time, to have your print ready the same day. Orders placed after 11:00 a.m. are treated as orders for the following day. Service is available Monday to Sunday.

What is the minimum Express print order?

The minimum for Express printing is 2 m². Send the dimensions and number of pieces so we can quote your order.

How much does Express service cost?

Express carries an additional fee over standard service. We confirm the total for your order before payment. If you need delivery, its cost is confirmed separately.

Where do I receive my order?

Collection is at our workshop in Cancun. Delivery through Uber or DiDi is also available, with a charge based on distance and time of day. Printing turnaround does not include transit time.

What do I need to send for a quote?

The product, dimensions, quantity, your file and required date. Let us know whether you will collect at the workshop or need delivery, and to which area.

What if my file is not ready?

Tell us what you have and what help you need when requesting a quote. We will review file preparation with you before confirming your order.
