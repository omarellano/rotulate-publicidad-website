FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our express printing services in Cancun

How fast is the delivery?

We offer guaranteed 24-hour delivery for most orders. Same-day printing is also available depending on our schedule and material availability.

Can you deliver to my resort or hotel?

Yes! We deliver directly to hotel lobbies, resort receptions, and event venues in Cancun, Costa Mujeres, Riviera Maya, and Tulum.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, Wire Transfers, and all major Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX) upon pickup or delivery.

How do I submit my print-ready files?

You can send us your files via WhatsApp or email. We accept PDF, Adobe Illustrator (.ai), and high-resolution JPEG/PNG formats.

Can you print materials in US standard sizes?

Yes! We work with both metric and imperial measurements (inches/feet) and support all standard US sizes for banners, step-and-repeats, and foam boards. We double-check your designs to make sure they fit correctly before printing.

How do I coordinate delivery to my hotel or wedding venue?

Simply provide your hotel/venue name, the name on the reservation (or event manager), and your target delivery time. We coordinate directly with the lobby or bellboys so your items are waiting for you when you arrive. We also work closely with local wedding planners and event coordinators.
